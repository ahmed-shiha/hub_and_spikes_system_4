# Reference: Member Churn Model (v1)

## 1. Business Context
It costs 5x more to acquire a new member than to keep an existing one.
MedIns wants to proactively call members who are unhappy *before* they cancel their policy.

## 2. The "Friction" Theory
People don't leave insurance for fun. They leave because of **Friction**:
1.  **Financial Friction:** Prices went up.
2.  **Administrative Friction:** Claims got denied.
3.  **Clinical Friction:** Access to care was blocked.

## 3. Detailed Feature Reference

### 3.1 `premium_increase_percentage`
*   **Logic:** (Current Premium - Last Year Premium) / Last Year.
*   **Insight:** "Sticker Shock". If a premium jumps 20%, churn probability spikes.

### 3.2 `manual_adjudication_rate`
*   **Source:** The STP Model.
*   **Logic:** % of this member's claims that went to `PEND`.
*   **Insight:** Pended claims take weeks to pay. This delay annoys members. A high rate indicates a poor customer experience.

### 3.3 `pre_auth_denial_rate`
*   **Insight:** "The doctor said I need surgery, the insurance said No." This is the highest form of emotional friction.

## 4. Technical Implementation
*   **Dependency:** This model cannot run independently. It requires the `abt_renewal.parquet` generated by the Pricing Model.
*   **Data Flow:** `Pricing Output` -> `Churn Input`.
*   **Training:** LightGBM Binary Classifier (`did_churn` 0/1).
